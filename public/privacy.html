FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK Privacy Commitment

FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED (“FS Cheap Fare Network”, “Company”, “Us”, or “We”) understands the importance of protecting your personal information and is dedicated to maintaining your trust. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, protect, maintain, and share your information when you visit our website and utilize our mobile booking services.

As an IATA Approved travel enterprise, we adhere strictly to international aviation data privacy standards, payment card industry standards (PCI-DSS), and regional consumer protection laws.

Consent

By using our website or utilizing any of our travel reservation services (including Flights, Hotels, Cruises, Trains, and Car Rentals), you agree to receive notifications and messages from FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED regarding your reservation, e-ticket issuance, schedule changes, and promotional fare alerts as per the policy outlined below. You must read and understand this Privacy Policy followed by our Terms and Conditions.

What Personal Information Do We Collect?

Information that you provide while subscribing to or completing travel bookings through our platform includes, but is not limited to:

  • Full Name & Contact Information: Legal passenger names, titles, and gender.
  • Email Address & Phone Number: For itinerary dispatch, status notifications, and customer service.
  • Permanent & Billing Address: Necessary for credit/debit card verification and fraud prevention.
  • Passenger Details: Date of birth, gender, nationality, and special meal or assistance requests.
  • Payment Credentials: Tokenized credit/debit card numbers, cardholder name, and billing zip code encrypted via SSL.
  • Travel Details: Departure dates, return dates, origin/destination hubs, cabin class, hotel room types, cruise itineraries, rail routes, or car rental preferences.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device identifier, and session activity logs.

How Do We Collect Your Personal Information?

We collect information across several interaction points:

  • When you fill out an online travel booking form or request telephone booking assistance from our agents.
  • Using cookies, pixels, and tracking technologies that record browsing behavior and travel search filters.
  • Through customer service calls placed to our toll-free reservation desk at +1 (888) 308-9004.
  • From authorized third-party providers including airline Global Distribution Systems (GDS), hotel aggregators, cruise lines, passenger rail carriers, and car rental partners.

Log Files

FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED follows standard web administration procedures regarding log files. These files collect Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, Internet Service Provider (ISP), browser types, referring/exit pages, operating systems, date and time stamps, and click counts. These log files are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely to analyze traffic patterns, administer the website, and prevent fraud.

Children's Privacy Protection (Age Restriction)

Our website is intended for individuals aged 18 or older and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent. If you believe your child has submitted information to our portal, please contact us immediately at support@fscheapfare.com or call +1 (888) 308-9004, and we will delete the data promptly.

Security of Your Data

We handle and collect all personal information securely using industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial procedures. All payment card transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) technology and processed through Level 1 PCI-compliant banking gateways. We never store raw credit card CVV codes on our servers.

CCPA Privacy Rights (California Consumers)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to request disclosure of personal information collected, request deletion of their data, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED does not sell personal consumer information to third parties.

GDPR Protection Rights (European Travelers)

For travelers located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, or export your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer.
