General Cancellation & Refund Rules

Travel booking services provided by FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED (including convenience fees, agency handling charges, and ticketing fees) are generally non-refundable per published airline fare rules and supplier tariffs.

All cancellation and refund requests must be initiated by speaking directly with our dedicated customer support desk via telephone at +1 (888) 308-9004. Electronic email notifications alone do not constitute confirmed cancellations until verified by an agent.

Acceptance of Refund Requests

FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED accepts refund requests only if all of the following conditions are met:

  • You have formally applied for cancellation and refund with our live customer service team.
  • The specific airline, hotel, cruise line, train operator, or car rental company fare conditions explicitly provide for cancellation and refund.
  • You are not marked as a "No Show" by the operating carrier (passengers marked as No Show forfeit all refund and exchange eligibility).
  • Applicable waivers or authorization codes are granted by airline suppliers to process the cancellation request.

24-Hour Flexible Cancellation Policy (Void Window)

If you cancel your flight reservation by calling our customer service team within 24 hours of booking (provided the booking was completed at least 7 days prior to scheduled departure), we evaluate your request under airline void-window guidelines. Cancellation fees and airline penalties apply after the expiration of this initial 24-hour void window.

Timeline for Processing Refunds

While timelines vary depending on airline financial institutions and payment card clearinghouses, refund processing typically takes 60–90 days calculated from the date of request initiation until funds reflect in your original payment card statement. All refunds are remitted solely to the original method of payment used during booking.

Exchange and Ticket Modification Policies

  • Confirmed non-refundable tickets cannot be refunded in cash, but in certain qualifying cases may be exchanged for a new ticket with the same operating airline for future travel dates within 365 days.
  • Airline penalty fees, agency exchange service fees, and any applicable fare differences apply to all ticket modifications.
  • Unused segments of partially flown tickets cannot be cancelled or refunded.
  • Changes in departure dates, passenger names, or route itineraries require up to 72 hours processing time with airline tariff desks.

Hotel, Cruise, Train & Car Rental Cancellations

  • Hotels: Cancellations made outside the property's penalty window are refunded per property guidelines. Prepaid non-refundable promotional rates are non-refundable.
  • Cruises: Marine cancellations adhere to cruise line voyage cancellation schedules and port fee refund regulations.
  • Trains & Rail: Passenger rail tickets are subject to national railway cancellation timetables and refund rules.
  • Car Rentals: Post-paid car rental reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to 48 hours prior to pickup.
