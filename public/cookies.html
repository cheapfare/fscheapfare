FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK Cookie Usage Commitment

FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK PRIVATE LIMITED uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to ensure that you enjoy the best possible browsing and booking experience. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files placed on your browser or device when you visit our website. They contain information that enables our systems to remember your active flight search inputs, travel dates, passenger counts, cabin class, hotel destination choices, and navigation preferences, curating a fast and seamless booking journey.

Types of Cookies Used by FS CHEAP FARE NETWORK

1. Session Cookies

Temporary cookies that expire and are automatically deleted when you close your web browser. These cookies are essential for maintaining your active flight booking session, locking fare quotes during checkout, and ensuring passenger information transfers accurately across booking steps.

2. Persistent Cookies

Cookies that remain on your device for a predetermined period or until manually cleared. These cookies store your preferred departure airport, language settings, and currency display preferences (USD default), allowing you to return to the website without reconfiguring your search filters.

3. Functional & Security Cookies

Critical cookies utilized to detect malicious activities, prevent automated bot attacks, verify SSL session tokens, and ensure complete PCI-DSS security compliance during payment checkout.

Why Do We Use Cookies?

  • Personalization: To remember your travel preferences, recent origin/destination airport searches, and customized flight filters.
  • Security & Verification: To protect your personal reservation data, detect unauthorized account interactions, and prevent fraudulent payment attempts.
  • Performance Analytics: To anonymously track site speed, server load, and funnel drop-off rates so our development team can optimize booking performance.
  • Relevant Travel Deals: To display contextual flight, hotel, cruise, rail, and car rental promotions that match your vacation interests.

How Can You Manage Your Cookie Preferences?

You have the complete legal right to accept, limit, or refuse cookies. Most modern web browsers allow you to modify cookie controls within their "Settings" or "Preferences" menu. You may configure your browser to notify you before a cookie is placed, delete existing cookies, or block third-party cookies altogether.

Please Note: Disabling essential session cookies may prevent you from locking airfares, maintaining active flight checkout steps, or completing reservations on our booking portal.
